# Source photograph and derivatives

Stephen Craven photographed *A hexagonal manhole cover* on 19 July 2013 in Newcomen Street, Southwark. The image is available from [Geograph](https://www.geograph.org.uk/photo/3739078) under [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/). The bundled source.jpg preserves the original JPEG.

The rectified textures, traced geometry, derived models A151, A152, A153 and M151, and their illustrations adapt this photograph. These derivatives are distributed under [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/). The derived assets use a reference width, designed thickness, hidden surfaces and specified cover poses. The inner lining texture uses the existing CC0 ambientCG Concrete049 input.
