# Traffic cone and road texture credits

The cone mesh and its textures are by **hinndia** and are available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Khronos adapted the model for its glTF material demonstration. [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/traffic-cone-573feef839d7450cb3e12da9986e7a98) · [Pinned upstream model, modifications and authors](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/f36bfdabd1031c3cf6689a50570b8cdf3678b49c/Models/TrafficCone)

RoadGS selects one cone from the demonstration, seats it upright or on its side, registers the two instances, packs the material channels, bakes sky visibility and samples the surfaces for native Gaussian rendering. These changes, the GLB geometry and the original creator are identified in the asset metadata. The accompanying renders and paired checkpoint incorporate this adapted cone.

The demonstration's asphalt textures are by **Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven**, released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02)
